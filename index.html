Frequently asked questions

How fast can a technician reach me?

Most Riyadh neighbourhoods are reached in under 45 minutes. Same-day service is available 24/7, including weekends and holidays.

Do I need a gas top-up or is something else wrong?

Weak cooling is often a refrigerant leak, but it can also be dirty coils or a failing capacitor. We test pressure first and only top up gas after finding and fixing any leak, so you don't pay twice.

When does a compressor need replacing?

If the unit hums but won't cool, trips the breaker, or the compressor is seized, replacement may be needed. We quote repair versus replacement upfront so you can choose.

Is there a guarantee on repairs?

Yes. Every repair carries a 30-day guarantee on labour and the fault repaired.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You approve the full price before work starts, with no surprise call-out or night-time charges.

Which units do you service?

Split, window, central/ducted ACs, water tank chillers and smart thermostat installs.
